# Brag Plan: Polform S.r.l.

## What is this app?
Polform S.r.l. is an industrial Jamstack showcase and B2B portal for a 27-year-old Southern Italian polyurethane conversion facility, featuring CNC contour cutting, technical packaging, acoustics, marine foams, and direct CAD inquiry bridges.

## The angle
Italian manufacturing heritage meets millimeter CNC precision. No corporate fluff, no generic stock footage: real technical polyurethane conversion from CAD drawings to certified foam geometries.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"MATERA · 1999" counter flashing into high-speed CNC wire contouring at ±0.5 mm tolerance. High-impact industrial typography on deep slate navy.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: From CAD to Foam.** 3D CAD drawing wireframe (.STEP / .DXF) cutting directly into physical open-cell and high-resilience foam shapes.
- **Beat 2: Multi-Sector Deployment.** Rapid burst through the 4 core sectors: Technical Protective Dunnage, Marine Reticulated Dryfeel, Convoluted Acoustic Panels, and Contract Upholstery.
- **Beat 3: Zero Switchboard.** Direct factory access via WhatsApp and phone (+39 0835 309182) — engineers talking directly to production.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero Middlemen. Direct Factory Floor." Polform logo lockup with live website link `darkrei08.github.io/polform`.

## User flow worth showing
CAD drawing upload (.STEP/.DXF) → CNC contouring calculation → instant quote bridge to Matera factory floor.
