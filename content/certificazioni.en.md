In precision polyurethane conversion, industrial credibility relies on **complete supply-chain traceability**. Polform sources exclusively certified raw foam blocks and supplies full documentation required for procurement contracts and international tenders.

---

## 1. Upstream Raw Material Certifications

Every polyurethane block transformed at Polform is produced by **primary European chemical manufacturers** accredited under leading environmental and safety standards:

* **CertiPUR® (EUROPUR Standard):**
  Guarantees that foams are manufactured without heavy metals (lead, mercury, cadmium), halogenated flame retardants, or phthalates, meeting strict European limits on volatile organic compounds (VOCs).
* **OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 (Class I & II):**
  Independent certification for skin-contact biocompatibility and chemical safety for nursery items, orthopedic pillows, and mattress toppers.

---

## 2. Flame-Retardant Compliance (Project-Based)

For contract furniture, hospitality, theaters, and marine applications, Polform processes certified fire-resistant foams:

* **Italian Class 1IM (UNI 9175):**
  Home Office fire reaction certification mandatory in Italy for hotels, auditoriums, public spaces, and healthcare facilities.
* **British Standard BS 5852 Crib 5:**
  Rigorous wooden-crib ignition test required for contract hospitality and yacht interiors in the UK, Middle East, and international luxury chains.
* **IMO FTP Code Part 8:**
  Marine flammability certification for cruise ships and commercial yachts.

---

## 3. CNC Cutting Tolerances & Quality Assurance

* **Dimensional Precision:** Computerized CNC contour and oscillating blade systems guarantee millimeter tolerances ($\pm0.5\text{ mm}$) for technical cases and dunnage trays.
* **Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS):** Full batch traceability and safety data sheets available upon request for each delivery.
* **Medical Devices CE Class I (EU MDR 2017/745):** Compliance dossiers for anti-decubitus cushions and rehabilitation positioning wedges.
