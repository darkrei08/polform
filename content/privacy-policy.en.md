## What we use your data for

We may handle your contact details, information about your request, and technical or CAD files you send us. We use them to reply, assess feasibility, and manage quotes and any related contractual relationship.

You can also contact us through WhatsApp links on this website. If you choose that channel, WhatsApp handles data under its own privacy notice.

## Providers and external services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

POLFORM uses an email service provider for business email. Public information about service logs and traffic data concerns metadata; it does not establish how long POLFORM keeps email contents or CAD files.

If you open a WhatsApp link, [WhatsApp's privacy notice](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy-eea) also applies.

## How long we keep data

We keep request data and related files for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting relationship. After that, we delete or restrict them unless we need to keep them to meet a legal obligation or protect a legal claim.

Mandatory accounting records follow statutory retention periods, normally 10 years. This does not mean that every CAD file is kept for that long.

## Website preferences and trackers

The website stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser's `localStorage` so it can remember your preference. The current website source does not include analytics tools or advertising trackers.

## Artificial intelligence

The current website does not offer an AI chatbot or make automated decisions about visitors. This statement is about the website only; it does not describe POLFORM's internal processes.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, or restrict the use of your data. Where the law provides, you can also object to processing or request data portability. Contact us at [info@polform.com](mailto:info@polform.com).

You can also complain to the [Italian Data Protection Authority](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/).
