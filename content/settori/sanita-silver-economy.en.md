## Pressure Relief & Decubitus Ulcer Prevention

Aging demographics and extended care require specialized composite foam geometries that alleviate pressure points without compromising postural stability.

### Healthcare Solutions
* **Dual-Layer Anti-Decubitus Cushions:** High Resilience (HR) base paired with pressure-relieving visco-elastic Memory Foam top layer for wheelchairs.
* **Transversely Profiled Hospital Mattress Cores:** CNC-cut transverse ventilation channels to reduce heat and moisture buildup.
* **Ergonomic Positioning Wedges:** Elevation wedges, lumbar rolls, and cervical positioning pillows for physical therapy and postoperative recovery.
* **Regulatory Conformity:** EU MDR 2017/745 Class I CE Medical Device compliance documentation available.
