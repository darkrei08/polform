## Continuous Innovation & Technical Co-Design

Alongside consolidated serial manufacturing, Polform collaborates with industrial R&D teams, engineering firms, and general contractors to pioneer **advanced applications for flexible polyurethane foam**. Our CNC machinery bridges 3D CAD models (.STEP, .DWG, .DXF) to physical evaluation prototypes within hours, allowing procurement teams to test densities, firmness, and clearances prior to serial tooling.

---

## Strategic Development Pillars

### 1. Aerospace, Avionics & Precision Sensor Protection
* **Industrial Context:** Proximity to the Southern Italian aerospace cluster (Matera, Grottaglie, Brindisi) creates demand for precision transport inserts protecting mission-critical avionics and sensors.
* **Solutions in Development:** Calibrated technical foam dunnage engineered to damp high-frequency vibration during air and ground transport of radar components, research drones, and optical payloads.

### 2. Circular Economy & Scrap Upcycling
* **Industrial Context:** Zero-waste manufacturing through high-value recovery of clean post-industrial virgin cutting scraps.
* **Solutions in Development:** Controlled granulation and steam-cured rebonding with solvent-free prepolymers, manufacturing **high-density bonded foam panels (up to 220 kg/m³)** for heavy machinery soundproofing and construction impact underlayments.

### 3. Advanced Postural & Ergonomic Prototypes
* **Industrial Context:** Engineering complex 3D contouring for uniform body pressure relief.
* **Solutions in Development:** Multi-density sandwich composites combining High Resilience (HR) structural cores with zoned visco-elastic memory foam toppers for wheelchair seating and specialized physiotherapy equipment.

### 4. Rapid Prototyping & Pilot Batches
* **B2B Service:** One-off custom samples and pilot runs (1–10 units) enabling design engineers to validate dimensional tolerances, jig fitment, and damping curves before committing to volume purchase orders.
