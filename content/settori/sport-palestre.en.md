## Kinetic Energy Dissipation & Athletic Safety

Athletic applications require progressive deceleration to protect joints and prevent impact trauma:

### Sports Products
* **Bouldering Crash Pads:** Dual-density composite landing mats (rigid load-spreading top layer + high-dissipation shock-absorbing base) for indoor climbing gyms.
* **Martial Arts Tatami Cores:** Ultra-high density bonded foam slabs (160–220 kg/m³) with calibrated surface stiffness for judo and karate.
* **Wall Impact Padding:** Soft protective wall mats for indoor soccer arenas, school gymnasiums, and soft play areas.
