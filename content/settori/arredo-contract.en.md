## Upholstered Elements for Public Venues & Luxury Hospitality

Furnishing commercial hotels, passenger cruise ships, and public auditoriums requires strict flammability compliance.

### Contract Solutions
* **Theater & Auditorium Seating:** Computerized wire-cutting of ergonomic anatomically profiled seats and backrests for cinemas and lecture halls.
* **Luxury Hotel & Resort Furnishings:** Italian Class 1IM fire-retardant foam cushions for headboards, banquettes, and lounge seating.
* **International Hospitality Standards:** British Standard BS 5852 Crib 5 compliance for UK and Middle East hotel projects.
