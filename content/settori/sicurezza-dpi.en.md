## Active Impact Protection & Industrial Compliance

Workplace ergonomics and industrial safety standards require reliable shock-absorbing padding:

### Industrial Safety Products
* **High-Visibility Bicolor Crash Bumpers (Yellow/Black):** Protective corner guards for building pillars, pallet racking uprights, and forklift loading bays.
* **Ergonomic PPE Cushioning Inserts:** Shock-absorbing knee pad inserts for craftsmen (floorers, tilers, mechanics) and helmet liner pads.
* **Reticulated Air Filter Foams:** Calibrated open-cell filter foams (10 to 60 PPI) for paint spray booths, air compressors, and HVAC ventilation systems.
