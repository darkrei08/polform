## High-Performance Marine Outdoor Cushioning

Continuous exposure to salt spray, rain, and UV quickly deteriorates conventional foams. Polform specializes in advanced reticulated marine foams:

### Technical Characteristics
* **Fully Open-Cell Reticulated Foam (Dryfeel Type):** 100% open honeycomb cell structure allows water to pass through freely via gravity, drying rapidly in sunlight and preventing mold, mildew, or odors.
* **Sanitized Biocidal Treatment:** Resistant to bacterial growth and marine fungus.
* **Fire-Rated Cabin Upholstery (IMO FTP Code Part 8):** Fully compliant with international maritime regulations for commercial cruise vessels and superyachts.
