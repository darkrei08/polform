## Reverberation Control & Industrial Acoustic Dampening

Environmental background noise and echo compromise productivity in offices and violate industrial machinery safety regulations.

### Polform Acoustic Solutions
* **Convoluted & Pyramidal Panels:** Surface profiling increases acoustic contact area, maximizing absorption coefficients across mid and high frequencies (NRC 0.65 – 0.85).
* **Architectural Acoustic Wall Panels:** CNC-shaped core panels upholstered with flame-retardant technical fabrics for corporate meeting rooms and restaurants.
* **Industrial Noise Enclosures:** Custom-cut soundproof linings for power generators, air compressors, pumps, and HVAC ducts.
* **Impact Sound Underlayment:** High-density bonded foam mats (80–140 kg/m³) for floor slab impact noise reduction in building construction.
