## Millimeter Protection for High-Value Equipment

Flexible polyurethane packaging absorbs shocks, drops, and temperature fluctuations during domestic and international transit.

### Capabilities & Typical Applications
* **Custom Airtight Case Inserts:** Precision inserts tailored for industry-leading protective case brands (Explorer Cases, GT Line, Peli Cases).
* **Industrial Dunnage Trays:** Custom-pocketed reusable transport trays for CNC-machined and polished components in automated manufacturing lines.
* **Luxury Wine & Spirits Packaging:** Graphite black protective shells for high-end wine and spirits international air shipments.
* **Precision Drone & Optics Enclosures:** Custom cavities for cameras, optical sensors, drones, and test equipment.
