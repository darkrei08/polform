## Heavy-Duty Vibration Damping & Custom Van Life Cushions

Industrial agricultural machinery and commercial vehicles demand foam grades engineered to withstand continuous mechanical vibration.

### Production Solutions
* **Tractor & Excavator Seating:** High-density foam contouring (40–55 kg/m³) with high creep resistance and low hysteresis loss.
* **Camper Van Conversions:** Precision-cut folding mattresses, bevelled lounge cushions, and pop-up roof beds tailored to European van conversions (Fiat Ducato, Ford Transit, Mercedes Sprinter).
* **Thermal & Acoustic Engine Insulation:** Adhesive-backed die-cut foam gaskets and bulkhead insulation panels.
