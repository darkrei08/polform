## Manufacturing Heritage in Southern Italy

**POLFORM S.r.l.** was established in **January 1999** in Matera (Southern Italy) by its founding partners, combining over a decade of prior operational and technical leadership within the largest polyurethane conversion plant in the Bari-Matera industrial district.

With more than **27 years of continuous production**, Polform couples artisan tactile mastery with computerized CNC cutting precision, supplying industrial components across Italy and Europe.

---

## Production Capabilities

* **CNC Contour & Wire Cutting:** High-speed oscillating blade and contour wire machinery for 2D/3D shapes from `.DXF`, `.DWG`, `.STEP`, and `.PDF` drawings with millimeter tolerances ($\pm0.5\text{ mm}$).
* **Splitting & Profiling:** Horizontal and vertical splitters, convoluted roller profiling for pyramidal acoustic panels and medical mattresses.
* **Bonding & Composite Lamination:** Solvent-free waterborne and hot-melt bonding benches for multi-density sandwiches, fiber batting (dacron), and technical textile backing.
* **Quality Assurance & Packaging:** Batch traceability, density verification, and roll-pack vacuum packaging to optimize international shipping volumes.
