**Visco-Elastic Memory Foam** reacts to body heat and weight, contouring gently to anatomical curvatures without capillary compression.

### Physical & Mechanical Properties

| Technical Parameter | Test Standard | Typical Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Apparent Density** | ISO 845 | 45 – 55 kg/m³ |
| **Indentation Hardness (40%)** | ISO 2439 | 1.2 – 1.8 kPa (Progressive soft) |
| **Recovery Time** | Internal test | 3 – 5 seconds |
| **Certifications** | — | CertiPUR, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I, MDR CE |

### Recommended Applications
* Wheelchair anti-decubitus cushions and nursing home mattresses.
* Pressure-relieving mattress toppers for luxury bedding.
* Ergonomic contoured cervical pillows.
