**High Resilience (HR)** polyurethane foam features an irregular open-cell microcellular structure that delivers progressive support and superior elasticity.

### Physical & Mechanical Properties

| Technical Parameter | Test Standard | Typical Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Apparent Density** | ISO 845 | 35 – 45 kg/m³ |
| **Indentation Hardness (40%)** | ISO 2439 | 3.2 – 4.5 kPa |
| **Ball Rebound Resilience** | ASTM D3574 | > 55 % |
| **Compression Set (75%, 22h, 70°C)** | ISO 1856 | < 6 % |
| **Certifications** | — | CertiPUR, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 |

### Recommended Applications
* High-traffic commercial seating and premium residential sofas.
* Anatomical seating for commercial vehicles, tractors, and camper vans.
* Supportive base cores for orthopedic and healthcare mattresses.
