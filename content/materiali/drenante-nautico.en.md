Engineered specifically to endure continuous rain, spray, and humidity. Its completely open reticulated cell matrix lets water drain through instantly via gravity.

### Physical & Mechanical Properties

| Technical Parameter | Test Standard | Typical Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Apparent Density** | ISO 845 | 30 – 35 kg/m³ |
| **Pore Count (Pores Per Inch)** | Optical count | 25 – 35 PPI |
| **Water Drainage Efficiency** | ASTM D3574 | Instant flow-through (100% open pore) |
| **Biocidal Treatment** | Sanitized | Antifungal and antibacterial |
| **Certifications** | — | IMO FTP Code Part 8 on request |

### Recommended Applications
* Boat deck sunbeds, flybridge seating, and rib cushions.
* Hotel poolside lounge chairs and exposed seaside patio furniture.
