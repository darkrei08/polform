Formulated with flame-retardant additives to resist ignition and self-extinguish when exposed to open flame sources in public venues.

### Physical & Mechanical Properties

| Technical Parameter | Test Standard | Typical Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Apparent Density** | ISO 845 | 30 – 50 kg/m³ |
| **Italian Homologation** | UNI 9175 | **Classe 1IM** (Home Office certified) |
| **British Standard** | BS 5852 | **Crib 5** (Wooden Crib Test) |
| **US Standard** | TB 117-2013 | Compliant |
| **Maritime Certification** | IMO FTP Code | Part 8 (Upholstered furniture) |

### Recommended Applications
* Cinema, theater, university, and public auditorium seating.
* Hospitality furniture, hotel headboards, and luxury cruise ship cabins.
