**Bonded Polyurethane Foam** is produced by granulating post-industrial virgin cutting scraps into uniform flakes, which are then compressed and steam-cured with eco-friendly polyurethane prepolymers.

### Physical & Mechanical Properties

| Technical Parameter | Test Standard | Typical Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Apparent Density** | ISO 845 | 80 – 220 kg/m³ |
| **Dynamic Stiffness (s')** | EN 29052-1 | 18 – 24 MN/m³ |
| **Impact Sound Improvement (ΔLw)** | EN ISO 140-8 | 26 – 32 dB (under screed) |
| **Certified Recycled Content** | GRS / CAM | > 85 % |
| **Flammability** | EN 13501-1 | Class E / Bfl-s1 treated |

### Recommended Applications
* Acoustic impact sound underlayment beneath floor screeds in commercial building construction.
* High-density cores for judo and martial arts tatami mats.
* Heavy-duty impact protective buffers for loading docks and logistics hubs.
