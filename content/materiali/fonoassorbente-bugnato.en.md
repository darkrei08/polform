The convoluted sinusoidal or pyramidal surface profile increases acoustic exposure area, dissipating sound wave energy via viscous air friction.

### Physical & Mechanical Properties

| Technical Parameter | Test Standard | Typical Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Apparent Density** | ISO 845 | 28 – 35 kg/m³ |
| **Standard Color** | Visual | Charcoal / Dark Graphite |
| **Noise Reduction Coefficient (NRC)** | ISO 354 | 0.65 – 0.85 (at 1000 – 4000 Hz) |
| **Available Thicknesses** | — | 30 mm, 40 mm, 50 mm, 70 mm |
| **Flammability** | UL 94 | HF-1 / Self-extinguishing |

### Recommended Applications
* Acoustic treatment for recording studios, control rooms, and podcast booths.
* Internal soundproofing linings for air compressor and motor housings.
