## Video direction

- Palette system: use `frame.md` roles — near-black ink and warm white canvas, Polform navy as structural accent, amber as the single action accent, muted slate for secondary text.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail settles (`power3` default), sequential reveals timed to each on-screen phrase, and held reads after each payoff. No front-loaded canvas dumps.
- Rhythm: Frames 1, 2, 3, and 6 carry the active motion; Frame 4 includes a deliberate still proof hold; Frame 5 is the calm breather before the CTA.
- Negative list: no fake machine footage, no generic AI gradients, no browser chrome or cursors, no unsupported performance claims, no lazy breathing loops, no screensaver-style floating elements, no slideshow front-load-then-freeze.
